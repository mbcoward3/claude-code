How the math works
  • Price path. The board sets one price per calendar year, so the model steps the price every January 1 by your growth assumption. A grant year with a real price you've entered anchors the path — later years grow from the most recent known price.
  • Vesting. 25% per year on September 30, first vest the year after the grant; each tranche rounds down to a whole share and the final date picks up the remainder.
  • Loan interest. Accrues daily (actual days ÷ 365) from September 30 of the grant year, first payment due December 31 of the following year — matching the disclosure's day count. Interest is due each December 31; unpaid interest capitalizes into principal at the capitalization rate. Principal is a single balloon due June 30, nine years after the grant.
  • Walk-away value. Vested shares × current price, plus unvested shares at your purchase price, minus every loan's payoff (principal + accrued interest) — i.e., what a departure redemption would net you.
  • Not modeled: taxes on gains at sale, payroll-deduct imputed interest, tax loans, share-exchange down payments, and minimum-redemption rules.
